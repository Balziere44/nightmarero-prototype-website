What the drop rates look like

  • 100%Trash loot, off every mob in the game
  • 1 to 5%Gear off ordinary mobs, nudged up a little below level 50
  • 10 to 50%Gear off Champions and MVPs
  • 1%Cards off ordinary mobs
  • 5%Cards off Champions and MVPs

There is no level based drop penalty, so an item never gets rarer because you out levelled the map it comes from.

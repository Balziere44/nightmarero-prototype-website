45 bosses

Bosses do not wander here. You call them

Every boss sits behind an altar on one map. To open it you hand over one of two lists. Option 1 is usually a pile of ordinary drops from that map. Option 2 is a handful of items that only champion monsters carry, marked in gold below. Plenty of altars offer just one of the two.

Altars, drop tables and relic gear documented by guild [SENAI]

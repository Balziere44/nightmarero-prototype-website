Spoilers

The answers, for when you want them

Half the fun here is not knowing. Several of these quests have no NPC pointing at them and no log entry, and working one out with friends is the point. So nothing below is open until you say so.

Found and worked out by Leo [SENAI]

Lighthalzen dungeon entrance Two ways in, neither of them signposted.

Amatsu field and dungeon Getting to Amatsu at all, then getting under it.

  1. To reach the Amatsu field you have to enter a house and take the hidden corridor. It is the same route as the official Ninja job quest.

  2. To open the dungeon, stand still in the middle of the Amatsu bridge for 30 seconds.

Fallen Hero The gate quest. Several other quests will not talk to you until this one is done.

  1. You need three pieces of Lord of the Dead gear: the armour, the cloak and the spear.

True Hero Shadow Gloves A long errand through Veins and Hugel for one piece of shadow gear.
  1. 1

    Go into the orphanage in Veins.

  2. 2

    Talk to the child downstairs and go through every dialogue option she has.

  3. 3

    Head outside, talk to the Old Lady and ask about Leela.

  4. After that conversation, pick the second option.

  5. 4

    Go back and talk to Leela again.

  6. 5

    Once those conversations are done, travel to Hugel and find her grandfather.

  7. 6

    Find Leela again and talk to her.

  8. 7

    Look for Garlindolf.

  9. 8

    Go to the Black Market, find the Smuggler at blk_mrkt 73,39 and choose "I need a person smuggled".

  10. 9

    Talk to Garlindolf again.

  11. 10

    Talk to the Smuggler again and pay him 25 Mysterious Card.

  12. 11

    Go to Veins and find the Shady Individual.

  13. 12

    Talk to Leela again.

  14. 13

    Talk to the Old Lady and distract her.

  15. Go through all three dialogue options with the old lady.

  16. 14

    Talk to the Shady Individual again.

  17. 15

    Go to the Black Market and talk to the Smuggler.

  18. 16

    Finally, go to Hugel and talk to Garlindolf.

  19. He hands over the True Hero Shadow Gloves.

Niflheim challenge dungeon The witch, the piano and two endings you can both take.

  1. Witch of Niflheim. You have to finish the Fallen Hero quest first.

  2. You need an Eye of Dullahan to get into the witch's mansion. Any monster can drop it, and champions seem to drop it more often.

  3. Once you have the accessory and are inside the mansion.

  4. She will not open the quest dialogue until Fallen Hero is finished.

  5. After that, talk to Kirkena. She asks for a book from the opera house and the remaining seven piano keys.

  6. Talk to the piano on the second floor of the mansion.

  7. The piano keys only appear while the Eye of Dullahan is equipped.

  8. Niflheim

  9. The witch's mansion

  10. nif_fild02

  11. nif_fild01

  12. Opera house, first floor

  13. Opera house, second floor

  14. Kill the boss in the Niflheim challenge dungeon and Kirkena opens up again, asking you to go back to the Fallen Hero. That unlocks his instance.

  15. Tell him he chose right and Eternal Regret is summoned.

  16. Tell him he chose wrong and Avenger of Tartaros is summoned.

  17. Bring ghost resistance.

  18. Once you have taken both choices, a third one appears: break the curse for good.

  19. Pick that and both bosses are summoned at once.

Thor challenge dungeon Buying your way past a metal gate.
  1. 1

    Interact with the metal gate at spot 3 in thor_v03.

  2. 2

    Talk to Rafeek in the Veins workshop.

  3. 3

    Go to the Black Market, ask the Information Dealer about the metal gate in Thor and pay 100 Mysterious Card.

  4. 4

    Talk to Rafeek again.

  5. 5

    Talk to the Smuggler in the Black Market. He asks for a few items.

  6. 6

    Talk to Rafeek once more and he hands over the key to the gate in thor_v03, which opens the Thor challenge dungeon.

Black Ops Collect the relics, hand in the intel, follow it to Aldebaran.

  1. Black Ops

  2. Farm the relics.

  3. Hand the intel to the Intelligence Officer. Once it is all in, pick the option to go back over the clues and he sends you to investigate Aldebaran.

  4. After the ship, talk to the Steel Wings Officer.

Potion crafting

Everything upgrades from the tier below

Potions are crafted and upgraded at the Alchemist Guild in Aldebaran. Each tier eats one of the tier under it, so the ladder starts at the Red Potion and works up.

This part comes from a player made wiki rather than from Twilight, and it was last touched in October 2024. Treat the amounts as a guide and check in game before you go farming.

Taekwon mission

Five rounds of boss hunting

The Taekwon Mission skill raises the job level cap, and it costs you the ability to change jobs ever again. These are the five rounds it asks for.

  1. 1

    Kill Moonlight Flower & Dracula

  2. 2

    Kill Goblin King & Osiris

  3. 3

    Kill Pharaoh & Kraken

  4. 4

    Kill Captain Sweety & Pyuriel

  5. 5

    Kill Rogue Prototype, Faceworm Queen & RSX-0806

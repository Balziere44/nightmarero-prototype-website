Loading screens

The art you stare at while the map loads

These are the loading screens that ship with the client, one per class, in full resolution. Click any of them to look properly, then take the file. They make decent wallpapers and nobody is going to stop you using them on Discord either.

Credits Artwork from Ragnarok Online 3 · edited in Photoshop by Balziere · class text written by Twilight.

Get the game client

Every file is a 1280 × 720 JPG, roughly 800 KB each. Free to keep, free to repost, just leave the credit alone.

14 screens

Inside the viewer the arrow keys move between screens and Esc closes it. The link in your address bar points at whichever one is open, so you can send someone a specific screen.

About the set

Where these came from

Why are some classes missing?
The set is being drawn one class at a time and this is everything that is finished. Seven transcendent classes and their seven third class counterparts are done so far, and the rest arrive as they get made.
Do I already have them?
Yes. They come with the client and rotate at random every time you change map, so you will meet all of them without downloading anything. This page just gives you the clean file.
Can I use them outside the game?
Wallpapers, Discord banners, guild recruitment posts, videos, go ahead. Keep the credit with them and do not sell them. This is a non commercial fan project and the underlying artwork is not ours to license.
The text on the card, is that in game data?
It is a summary of how the class actually plays here, written for this server, so it is a fair description of what you get. The skill numbers themselves live on the class pages and in the wiki, which are the places that stay current.
Artwork
Character art from Ragnarok Online 3, the sequel that never shipped. It is the cleanest source of high resolution art for these classes that exists.
Composition
Cut out, relit, recoloured and laid out in Photoshop by Balziere, with the parchment panel and the class emblems built to match the rest of the client.
Words
The class summary and the tag line on every screen were written by Twilight, based on how the class plays after the rebalance.

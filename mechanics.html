The rules

How the server actually works

Everything below is different enough from the game you remember that guessing will cost you levels. It is worth twenty minutes before you pick a build.

Levels and experience

The tables were rewritten from scratch, so nobody can quote you a rate. What follows is the shape of the curve instead.

Max level
150 base, 50 job. Jobs come in three tiers: first job carries you from 1 to 50, second from 51 to 99, third from 100 to 150. Each tier has its own fifty job levels, so fifty skill points to spend before you move on.
Ninja and Gunslinger
The exceptions. They stay on their first job until 99 and then go straight to their final one. They still collect both sets of fifty skill points.
Per kill
About 5% of a level at base 50, 1% at 100, 0.1% at 149. Monsters here take much longer to kill than you are used to, so read those numbers with that in mind.
Parties
Five players. Experience only splits evenly if the lowest and highest member are within 15 levels of each other.

What the level gap costs you

Your level against the monsterExperience
15 or more levels above the monsterNothing at all
10 levels aboveHalf
6 levels above90%
Up to 5 levels aboveFull experience
3 to 10 levels below5% more, rising to 40% more at ten
10 to 15 levels belowTapering back down, 15% more at fifteen
16 or more levels below90% less

So the sweet spot is monsters a few levels above you, not below. Fighting something ten levels higher pays 40% more, and grinding something fifteen levels lower pays nothing.

Stats and breakpoints

Each stat still does what you expect, and then gives you something extra at every tenth and every twenty fifth point. Several of those bonuses depend on what you are holding.

  • Every 25Melee damage +3%

Bow, instrument, whip or gun

  • Every 10Defence pierce +3%
  • Every 25Max HP +3%

  • Every 25All skill cooldowns -5%

  • Every 25Potion cooldown -0.5s

  • Every 25Magic damage +3%

Dex

Dexterity

Physical attack with ranged weapons, hit rate, and cast time.

Bow, instrument, whip or gun

  • Every 10Weapon attack +5%
  • Every 25Ranged damage +3%

Any other weapon

  • Every 25Movement speed +3%
Luk

Luck

Critical rate and perfect dodge.

  • Every 10Outgoing status duration +3%
  • Every 255% chance to resist most negative statuses

Bonus stats count. If you have 77 Strength and 23 from gear and buffs, the game reads 100 for every breakpoint above. The Luck resistance roll does not cover statuses you inflict on yourself, item or homunculus cooldowns, Threefold Arrow or Quick Draw charges, Convex Mirrors, or Death Sentence.

Combat maths

Most formulas match renewal. These are the ones that do not, and they are the ones that decide how a build feels.

Critical hits
Every physical skill and attack can crit. Rate is still 1 + (Luk / 3) + (base level / 100). The damage bonus dropped from 40% to 20%, crits no longer skip flee or defence, and monsters have no crit shield, so their Luck does not reduce your rate.
Cast time
Split into fixed and variable. Variable reduction is sqrt((Dex × 2) / 600) × 100, which means 300 Dex for a full 100%, which nobody reaches. Reduction from gear adds to reduction from stats instead of multiplying, so 40% from stats and 60% from gear leaves nothing. Fixed cast only bends to gear and to the Intelligence breakpoints.
Attack speed
ASPD = base ASPD + (Agi / 5), where the base comes from your class and weapon type. There is a soft cap at 185 that gear and buffs can push through, and a hard cap at 190 that nothing can.
Status resistance
Outside the Luck breakpoints, no stat resists anything. Two thousand Vitality still gets stunned. Gear is the only other answer.
Elements and cards
Weapon element applies to your whole attack, not just the weapon part of it. Damage bonuses against a race, element or size work the same way.
Movement and regen
Base movement went from 150ms per cell to 120ms. HP and SP regeneration are five times what you remember.

Holy beats everything and is beaten by nothing except itself, which is why holy monsters are the wall they are. Poison is a small edge against most things and a wall against Undead. Neutral has no answer to Ghost.

Status effects

Statuses are a pillar of combat here rather than a nuisance. Most last five seconds or less and hit far harder than they used to, and a lot of skills change behaviour depending on what is already on the target.

  • Bleeding Damage every second, scaled off the applier's Strength and level. Internal Bleeding works the same but scales off Dexterity. Internal Bleeding, Bleeding, Bleed
  • Burning Damage every second, scaled off Intelligence. Severe Burning scales off Vitality instead. Severe Burning, Burning
  • Poison Damage every second, scaled off Agility. Deadly Poison scales off Luck instead. Deadly Poison, Poison
  • Blind Cuts the target's Hit and Flee by a flat 30, and shrinks how much of the screen a player can see. Blinded, Blind
  • Vulnerable Strips up to 40% of the target's hard and soft defence. No attack bonus, unlike the Provoke you remember. Vulnerable
  • Breach The same idea aimed at magic defence instead. It is how a caster gets through a high MDef enemy, and Breach Potency raises what it strips. Breached, Breach
  • Frozen Cancels casting and locks the target until it takes damage. Chill slows movement and attack speed, Cold is the follow up during the resist window. Freeze, Frozen, Chill
  • Stun Cancels casting and stops the target acting. Monsters resist for 30 seconds afterwards and take Stagger instead. Staggered, Stagger, Stunned
  • Sleep Cancels casting and stops the target acting until it takes damage. Monsters take Dazed during the resist window. Sleeping, Dazed, Sleep
  • Slow Movement speed down. Atrophy hits attack speed instead, and Slowcast stretches fixed cast time. Immobilized, Immobilize, Slowcast

Two rules follow from that. No stat gives you any resistance, so the only defences are the Luck breakpoints and gear. And champions and bosses have no immunity at all, so everything you carry works on them.

Potions and catalysts

Your potion is a piece of equipment, not a stack of items. It is never consumed, it sits on a ten second cooldown, and you upgrade it as you go.

Healing
Red, then Orange, Yellow, White. Around level 30 the Red one stops keeping up. Gather the herbs and materials and commission the upgrade from the Alchemists in Aldebaran, or from any starting town tool shop for the Red one.
SP
Grape Juice, then Blue Potion.
Attack speed
Concentration, Awakening, Berserk. Every class restriction on these has been removed.
Status
A Green Potion, for when the pressure gets silly.
Catalysts
Most class consumables are gone. The ones that stayed became catalysts that are not used up: endow stones, arrows, maintenance kits, poisons and the rest. Short quests or crafting get you them.

The three you have to earn

SkillWhat it does
ResurrectionRevives a downed player and spends one Holy Water out of their inventory. You get three every time you respawn or talk to a Kafra.

All three need a quest to learn and more quests to raise past level 1. Where each one starts is written in its skill description, in your skill tree.

Refining

The one part of this server that is kinder than the game you remember. Gear never breaks and never drops a level. The only thing at risk is your wallet.

The odds
Maximum is +10, and success is 100% minus 10% per current refine. So +0 to +1 always works, +4 to +5 is a coin flip and a bit, and +9 to +10 is one in ten.
One weapon level
Every weapon in the game refines with the same ores. Armour, shields, garments, shoes and headgear share one ladder too.
Where the ores come from
Mostly from killing bosses, banded by the boss's own level.

Warps

The Kafra teleport service works, with one condition attached.

  • You cannot warp to a town until you have walked there once and spoken to its Kafra.
  • Prontera, Izlude, Morroc, Geffen, Payon and Alberta are open from your first login, and warping between those six is free.
  • Everything else costs zeny once you have unlocked it.
  • Unlocks are account wide, so your second character starts with everything the first one opened.

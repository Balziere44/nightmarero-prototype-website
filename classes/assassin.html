Assassin

Second tier Base level 51 to 99 Thief line

The Assassin is the primary advancement option for the Thief class. While the Thief's skill tree was very much defensively minded, the Assassin uses that foundation to fill their skill bar with offensive options.

The core of the Assassin kit lies in their Blade Enchants. While each Enchant adds its own passive ability to the Assassin, they also serve to enhance all of the Assassin's damage skills in various ways. While only one Blade Enchant can be active at a time, the short cooldown on them makes swapping between them as the situation dictates very possible, giving the Assassin incredible versatility in the field.

As for their actual damage skills, these are divided into two general categories: Katar skills, focusing on Critical Hits and high burst damage, and Dagger skills, focused on delivering a consistent stream of damage and increased mobility.

Finally, the Assassin draws on its roots for two defensive skills, Cloaking and Hallucination Walk. The former is a short term invisibility skill, allowing the Assassin to weave in and out of combat as necessary, while the latter gives an impressive layer of defense, maxing the user's Perfect Dodge for a short time to help them squeak out of exceptionally dangerous situations.

Soul Ascetic

Third tier Base level 100 to 150 Taekwon line

Soul Ascetic is the alternative advancement option for Soul Linkers. While Soul Reapers turn their efforts toward studying and mastering the evil spirits that plague the world, Soul Ascetics instead focus on communing with the spirits of the natural world, turning their otherwise aimless, but still very potent, power toward good.

A Soul Ascetic's bond to these spirits of nature is not a given, though. It is a tenuous balance that they must struggle to maintain at all times. This manifests itself through the Soul Ascetic's AP. This value always rests between 0 and 200, but as it approaches the exact center of that range, the Soul Ascetic's abilities become much more powerful. Or from another perspective, one most Soul Ascetics will tend to adopt themselves, the closer their AP comes to the extremes of its spectrum, the weaker their performance.

There are two primary ways the Soul Ascetic manages this balance. The first is through the use of Rituals, two stance-like abilities that immediately give or take a large amount of AP, on top of providing a short duration buff.

In addition to these Rituals, all Soul Ascetic abilities modify the user's current AP value, either granting or draining AP. Balancing these gains and losses with the Rituals, and knowing when to throw out more Soul Ascetic skills and when to hold them in reserve and rely on your Soul skills forms the core of the Soul Ascetic playstyle.

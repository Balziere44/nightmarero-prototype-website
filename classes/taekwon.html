Taekwon

First tier Base level 1 to 50 Taekwon line

The Taekwon is the first of the Expanded Classes, a group of classes outside the core six starter classes that sport somewhat more complicated class mechanics than usual, and may have somewhat abnormal class structures.

The Taekwon continues its vanilla tradition of spurning weapons in favor of a mastery that provides considerable amounts of Attack very early in the game, though they shouldn't put away those weapon cards quite yet. While they can't equip weapons, they can instead equip "Card Albums," equipment that take the Weapon Slot of the equipment window, but have no effect at all except to carry Weapon cards. This allows them to punch, or I suppose kick, above their weight class with a bit of investment, sparing them the fate of being left behind by other jobs who continue to gain stronger and stronger weapons as they progress.

Taekwon skills can be divided into two broad categories: Martial Skills and Kick Skills. The Martial Skills are all passive. They each associate with a single core stat (Strength, Agility, Dexterity, etc), increasing that stat and providing a related bonus. These give the Taekwon a strong core that can build themselves to a competitive level against other jobs without even having to consider active skills.

In addition to these passives are the Kick Skills, which function quite differently than those in vanilla. Instead of requiring a stance proc to activate, the Kick skills can all be activated freely, and will instead provide a stance buff upon doing so. While a stance buff is active, it will provide buffs to all following kicks that are used , until the user attempts to double up. The moment a kick whose stance is already active is used, or when the five second timer on the first stance expires, all stance buffs are immediately removed, and the combo must be rebuilt from scratch. This allows skilled Taekwons to "build their own combos," optimizing their rotations for each specific encounter.

Finally, Taekwons have one more unique characteristic that sets them apart even from other Expanded classes: instead of changing jobs to Star Gladiator or Soul Linker when they reach Job Level 50, they can instead opt to take on the "Taekwon Mission." This series of quests lock them out of job changing permanently, and in exchange grant them powerful buffs to all of their skills, a new kick inaccessible to ordinary Taekwons, and fifty additional job levels to fill out the remainder of their skill tree.

Skill list

Skills

Skill names and effects are kept in English because that is how they appear in game.

BleedingBlindVulnerableSlowWhat these do
  • Martial Training

    Training in basic combat and physical fitness. Passively increases the user's Strength and Hit Rate, and increases the user's damage while unarmed.

  • Martial Endurance

    Training in endurance and perseverance. Passively increases the user's Vitality and increases the effectiveness of Healing Potions they use.

  • Martial Discipline

    Training in focus and discipline. Passively increases the user's Intelligence and increases the effectiveness of SP Potions they use.

  • Martial Precision

    Training in precise and accurate technique. Passively increases the user's Dexterity, Hit Rate, and Defense Pierce. Defense Pierce gained from this skill is treated as card-like pierce for stacking purposes.

  • Martial Agility

    Training in rapid and flexible movement. Passively increases the user's Agility, Flee, and Perfect Dodge.

  • Counter Kick

    Delivers a powerful kick to the enemy, ignoring the target's Flee and bypassing skills like Guard. Activating this skill triggers Counter Stance for 5 seconds, during which all Kick skills ignore the target's Flee and bypass skills like Guard. Counter Stance ends when any other Stance ends, or when Counter Kick is cast again.

  • Tornado Kick

    The user delivers a wide-reaching kick that strikes all enemies in an area around them. Activating this skill triggers Tornado Stance for 5 seconds, during which all Kick skills splash in an area around the target. Tornado Stance ends when any other Stance ends, or when Tornado Kick is cast again.

  • Axe Kick

    The user delivers a staggering blow to the top of the enemy's head, dealing significant damage that will leave the target briefly stunned. Activating this skill triggers Axe Stance, during which all Kick Skills inflict various status effects to their targets. Axe Stance ends when any other Stance ends, or when Axe Kick is cast again.

  • Counter Kick: Inflicts Vulnerable
  • Tornado Kick: Inflicts Blind
  • Roundhouse Kick: Inflicts Slow
  • Demolishing Kick: Inflicts Bleeding
  • Roundhouse Kick

    The user delivers a spinning roundhouse kick to an enemy, utilizing their momentum to keep attacking at a breakneck pace. Activating this skill triggers Roundhouse Stance for 5 seconds, during which the user's ASPD is increased. Roundhouse Stance ends when any other Stance ends, or when Roundhouse Kick is cast again.

  • Taekwon Mission

    Quest Skill. In exchange for permanently blocking the ability to change jobs, increases the Job Level cap of the user (by up to 50) and increases the damage of all Kick Skills except Demolishing Kick.

  • Demolishing Kick

    The user unleashes a kick with devastating force, dealing exceptional damage to an enemy based on the number of currently active Stances. All Stances immediately end upon casting this skill. This skill cannot be learned unless Taekwon Mission is unlocked.

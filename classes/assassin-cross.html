Assassin Cross

Third tier Base level 100 to 150 Thief line

Assassin Cross is the primary advancement option for Assassins. In contrast to the Guillotine Cross, which devotes its entire arsenal to the perfection and use of a variety of poisons, the Assassin Cross seeks to perfect their own martial skill. Following the same core principles as the Assassin before them, the Assassin Cross gains a variety of Weapon-based skills that allow them to dictate the flow of any encounter.

Dagger skills revolve around the Weapon Blocking skill. Weapon Blocking protects the user from any one physical attack, entirely nullifying its damage and giving the user the Counter State buff. While in this state, the other Dagger skills used by the Assassin Cross have their effects enhanced and their cooldowns refreshed. The tree concludes with Savage Blade, a ranged attack that proactively triggers the Counter State to allow fearsome pushes on the user's own timing.

The Katar skills have a much different focus: deal damage fast. These skills revolve around Sonic Acceleration, a buff that increases the Attack Speed, Max Attack Speed, and reduces the Cooldowns of all other katar skills while active. This allows the Assassin Cross to bring down enemies with a swift flurry of skills or a lightning-speed barrage of autoattacks, whichever the situation demands.

The two trees are supplemented by a triplet of generic skills useful in any build. Peerless Blades is added to the lineup of Blade Enchants, granting bonuses based on how high the user is able to keep their HP. Deadly Shadow offers another way to access the Cloaking state, and grants bonus Defense Pierce and Crit Damage to the first skill used afterward. Finally, Soul Destroyer turns the user's active Blade Enchant into a deadly projectile, consuming it to do enormous damage with a variety of properties based on which Blade Enchant was chosen.

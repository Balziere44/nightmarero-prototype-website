Maboroshi

Third tier Base level 100 to 150 Ninja line

Maboroshi, previously known as Shinkiro and Shiranui, is the alternative advancement option for Ninjas. While the Kagemusha focuses on perfecting the use of their original Ninja techniques, learning advanced ways to make use of their original three Ninja Arts, the Maboroshi decides that original Ninja skills weren't complicated enough, and decides to kick things up a notch.

While Maboroshi skills can be divided into three branches to match those of the original Ninja skills, Melee, Ranged, and Magic, they add another layer of complexity on top of these three basic arts. All of the Maboroshi kit is built around the "Mirage" mechanic, an ability they gain with their new skills to leave illusionary clones of themselves behind when they use certain skills.

Unlike their namesake, these Mirages can in fact be quite deadly. Every Maboroshi skill will trigger these Mirages to attack, the skill they use being determined by which Ninja Arts the user currently has active. With up to three of these Mirages capable of taking to the field at once, Shinkiro and Shiranui will find themselves faced with a mind boggling level of flexibility in how they approach any encounter.

This unprecedented level of flexibility fuses with an exceptional array of mobility skills, both from their original Ninja skills and the new Mirage-generating skills they gain access to after job change. A master Maboroshi will be like a flickering mirage on the battlefield themselves, striking everywhere yet being nowhere, leaving their enemies bewildered and helpless.

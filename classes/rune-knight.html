Rune Knight

Third tier Base level 100 to 150 Swordsman line

Rune Knight is the alternative advancement option for Knights. While the Lord Knight continues the orthodox style of the Knight, expanding its stable rotations with more skills that function in much the same way, the Rune Knight takes an entirely different direction.

Rather than a skill tree, the Rune Knight's skills are more accurately described as a "Skill Pool." With virtually no prerequisites for their skills, they are free to select any skills they wish right from the start. This turns their third job skills into a versatile toolbox that can be selectively applied to enhance the user's own particular style of play.

The skills available for Rune Knight are evenly distributed between direct attack skills and self buffs. Some of these slot perfectly well into the original rotational gameplay of the Knight, but others demand an entirely new playstyle to appreciate. In particular, all Rune Knight attack skills benefit heavily from Int investment, making stat point distribution a bit more challenging than it was previously for them.

In addition to this toolbox of skills, the Rune Knight has the unique ability to craft Runes. These are single-use items that function in the same manner as Cards, able to be slotted into equipment and used by any class. Furthermore, each Rune comes with a unique bonus that can only be used by Rune Knights.

Each variety of Rune comes in five levels, each progressive level giving a stronger bonus but requiring more difficult materials to create. There are ten varieties of Rune available at present.

Core Skills2 skills

  • Rune ResearchPassiveLv 10

    Boosts the effectiveness of all Rune Knight skills.

    Research into the nature and use of Rune Magic. Increases the user's Int and enhances the effectiveness of all Rune Knight skills. Has no effect on the creation of Rune Stones, or their effectiveness after creation.

  • Rune InscriptionCraftingLv 10

    Enables crafting of slottable, single-use Rune Stones.

    Study into the creation of Rune Stones, single-use items that can be slotted into equipment in the same manner as cards. Runes have a level from 1 to 5. The maximum level of Rune that can be created is based on the level of Rune Inscription learned.

    Created Runes can be used by any class, but gain additional effects based on the Rune Inscription level of the character equipping them.

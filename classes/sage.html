Sage

Second tier Base level 51 to 99 Mage line

Sage is the secondary advancement option for Mages. While the Wizard focuses mainly on turning the control options of Mage into large amounts of damage, the Sage doubles down on the control aspect of their first job, using their new skills to further restrict their enemies' movements, all while dealing consistent damage over a wide area.

The Sage builds on its first three elemental skill choices with two new skills each: one to enhance their movement or restrict the movement of enemies, and another that places a large ground-based AoE that deals constant damage over time to all enemies standing within it. Beyond this, the Sage must leverage its first job options to put out competitive amounts of damage.

But damage is not all the Sage offers. It also has access to a plethora of support and utility skills. Endow Weapon gives party members a chance to swap their element without having to rely on costly high-end cards, while also buffing the magic damage of that element used by magic users. Endow Armor does the opposite, giving high levels of resistance to a given element at the cost of reduced resistance from its opposite. Both skills require a catalyst crafted at the Sage Castle to be used to their full effect, but when used without one, can also serve to protect party members from enemies maliciously attempting to change their weapon and armor elements.

More selfishly, the Sage has access to Transposition Spell, a simple ability that allows them to teleport to any location within line of sight, and Spell Eater, a defensive skill that entirely nullifies single-target magic aimed at them. When successful, the user gains a buff that enables them to cast their next spell instantly, ignoring both Variable and Fixed Cast Times.

Finally, the Sage has access to Antimagic Field, a powerful land spell that gives a mind-boggling amount of magic resistance to anyone who stands on it, in addition to conferring immunity to the Burning, Chilled, and Sleep statuses. However, the key is anyone. Both enemies and allies alike will enjoy the bonuses that the field provides, forcing the Sage to be very careful about their positioning to make good use of the skill without neutering their own damage output.
